# Groundschool AI — 30-Day LinkedIn Content Schedule (v2)

## Strategy Overview

**Who you are:** A student pilot who also codes. You got frustrated with the exam prep options in SA, so you started building your own tool. That's the whole story.

**Voice:** Talk like you're updating a friend. First person, casual, honest. Not a startup pitch. Not a SaaS founder. Just a person sharing what they're working on and what they're learning along the way.

**Posting cadence:** 5 posts/week (weekdays). Consistency matters more than perfection.

**Content mix:**
- Your journey as a student pilot and builder
- Things every student pilot relates to
- What you're building and how it's going
- Stuff you've learned that might help others study

**Visual format:** Screen recordings of the app, screenshots, behind-the-scenes of your study setup. No face required.

**On selling:** Don't. Seriously. For the first 2 weeks especially, just share and be a person. People will ask about the app if what you're sharing resonates. From Week 3 on, you can mention the link casually — "link in comments if anyone's curious." Never hard sell.

### Day 7 (Tuesday) — STUDY TIP
**Caption:**
```
A study technique that's been working really well for me:

Read the chapter once. Don't highlight everything. Just read it.

Then close the book and write down everything you can remember.

Compare what you wrote to the actual content. The gaps are where you need to focus next time.

It's called active recall. The idea is that trying to remember something is what actually makes it stick. Way more effective than re-reading the same page five times.

It's also why practice questions work so well. You're forcing your brain to retrieve information, which is exactly what the exam is going to ask you to do.
```
**Visual:** Simple Canva graphic with the steps, or a photo of your notes.
**Notes:** Zero app mention. Pure value. The kind of post people save and share.

## General Tips

1. **Post between 7-9am SAST** — LinkedIn engagement peaks in the morning
2. **Reply to every comment** within the first hour — the algorithm rewards conversations
3. **Never put links in the main post** — LinkedIn buries posts with external links. Always say "link in comments" and drop it there
4. **Use line breaks** — short paragraphs, lots of white space. Nobody reads walls of text on a phone
5. **Screen recordings: 15-45 seconds max** — shorter is better
6. **Hashtags go in the first comment**, not the post. Use: #StudentPilot #Aviation #SACAA #PilotLife #BuildingInPublic #SouthAfrica
7. **Comment on other people's posts daily** — instructors, flight schools, aviation pages. This grows your network faster than posting alone
8. **If a post flops, don't delete it** — not every post will perform. Consistency beats virality every time
9. **Don't overthink it** — an imperfect post that goes up is better than a perfect post that stays in your drafts
